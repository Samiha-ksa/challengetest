    How it works
    1. Save your name, then tap the deck to flip a card and do that many squats.
    2. Tap the deck again for the next card. The card you just finished counts as cleared.
    3. Tap "I'm out" when you can't finish a card. That card does not count.
    4. The deck is shuffled once with a fixed seed, so everyone gets the same cards in the same order.
